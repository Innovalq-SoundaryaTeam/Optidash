Diagnostic Care

Comprehensive Clinical Eye Exam

Unmatched Optical Precision.

Our Comprehensive Eye Exam goes far beyond the standard visual acuity test. Utilizing a suite of advanced digital diagnostics, we map the exact topography of your retina and measure intraocular pressures with zero physical contact.

This allows our master optometrists to not only pinpoint your perfect prescription, but to detect early signs of conditions like glaucoma, macular degeneration, and digital eye strain long before they affect your vision.

What's Included in Your Visit:

  • Digital Refraction & Prescription Determination
  • Ocular Pressure Screening (Glaucoma Test)
  • Optos Retinal Health Analysis & Imaging
  • Personalized Lens & Frame Consultation

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the exam take?

A typical exam takes between 20 to 30 minutes depending on the necessary diagnostic imaging.

Do I need to dilate my eyes?

Because we use Optos digital retinal imaging, dilating drops are completely optional for the vast majority of our patients.
