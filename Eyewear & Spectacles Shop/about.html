Our Mission

Founded in 2010, OptiDash was created with a simple premise: premium clinical eye care and architectural, stylish frames shouldn't be mutually exclusive. We believe that glasses are the most important accessory you wear.

"To bring absolute clarity to your vision and uncompromising confidence to your style."

The Journey

2010

First boutique clinic opened in the design district, featuring independent frame makers.

2018

Launched in-house lens manufacturing facility to ensure military-grade precision for every prescription.

2026

Nationwide expansion and launch of the proprietary OptiDash OS clinic management platform.
