Shop FAQs

Can I use my existing prescription when ordering?

Yes — upload a valid prescription at checkout, or visit any OptiDash store for a free eye test before ordering.

What is your returns and exchange policy?

Return or exchange any frame within 14 days of delivery, provided it's unused and in original packaging. Prescription lenses are non-returnable once fitted, but can be remade if there's a power mismatch.

Are contact lenses available without a prescription?

No. All contact lenses, including colored lenses, require a valid prescription for your safety. Our optometrists can issue one during an in-store eye test.
