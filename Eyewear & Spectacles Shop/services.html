Service FAQs

Do I need an appointment for frame styling?

While walk-ins are always welcome, we highly recommend booking an appointment for our bespoke frame styling. This ensures you receive dedicated, one-on-one time with our expert stylists to find your perfect architectural fit.

Do you accept out-of-network insurance?

Yes, we work with most major vision insurance providers. We can provide a detailed, itemized receipt for you to submit directly to your insurance company for out-of-network reimbursement.

How long does it take to get my glasses?

Since all of our lenses are custom-crafted and index-matched, standard single-vision orders typically take 5-7 business days. Complex prescriptions or progressive lenses may take 10-14 business days to ensure absolute optical precision.
